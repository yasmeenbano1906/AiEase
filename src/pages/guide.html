01 • WHAT IS AI

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, and making decisions.

🧠

Learns From Data

AI analyzes large amounts of data to discover patterns, improve accuracy, and make predictions.

⚡

Makes Decisions

AI evaluates information and selects the most suitable action or response.

🤖

Automates Tasks

AI performs repetitive work faster, reducing human effort and increasing productivity.

💡

Solves Problems

AI helps solve complex real-world problems in healthcare, finance, education, and many other industries.

📊

Finds Patterns

AI identifies hidden relationships in data that humans might overlook.

🚀

Improves Over Time

Machine Learning enables AI systems to become smarter through experience and feedback.

03 • TYPES OF AI

Major Types of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence includes several specialized fields, each designed to solve different kinds of real-world problems.

🧠

Machine Learning

AI learns patterns from data and improves its predictions without explicit programming.

Example: Netflix Recommendations
🤖

Deep Learning

Uses multi-layer neural networks to solve complex recognition and prediction tasks.

Example: Self-Driving Cars
💬

Natural Language Processing

Enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

Example: ChatGPT
👁️

Computer Vision

Allows AI to analyze and understand images, videos, and visual information.

Example: Face ID
🎨

Generative AI

Creates new content including text, images, music, videos, and code.

Example: Midjourney
🦾

Robotics

Combines AI with machines to automate physical tasks in the real world.

Example: Warehouse Robots

06 • PROMPT ENGINEERING

Write Better Prompts, Get Better Results

A prompt is the instruction you give to an AI model. The clearer your prompt, the more accurate and useful the AI's response will be.

❌

Weak Prompt

Write code.

The request is too short and lacks important details, so the AI has to guess what you need.

✅

Effective Prompt

Build a responsive login page using HTML, CSS and JavaScript with modern UI and form validation.

A detailed prompt provides context, requirements, and the expected outcome, helping AI generate higher-quality results.

Tips for Better Prompts

①

Be Specific

Clearly explain exactly what you want AI to do.

②

Add Context

Tell AI your goal, audience, or situation.

③

Define Output

Mention the format like table, code, list, article, or explanation.

④

Refine

Continue the conversation and improve the answer step by step.
