What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is one of the most important technologies shaping the modern world.

In simple terms, AI refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Why does AI matter?

AI can help people analyze information, generate content, solve problems, automate repetitive tasks, and make better decisions.
